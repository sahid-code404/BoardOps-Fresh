# Domain inventory

Identity & Registration; Sessions & 2FA; Institution; Authorization; Residents; Meals; Leave & Guest Meals; Kitchen; Products & Procurement; Expenses; Resident Funds; Payments; Refunds & Adjustments; Variables; Formula Engine; Billing Cycles; Monthly Snapshots; Bills; Ledger; Monthly Closing; Notifications & Announcements; Reports/Exports; Settings & Policies; Holidays/Calendar; Audit; Background Tasks/System Administration.

Each domain receives a single authoritative server-side implementation. UI modules may format/display results but must not become duplicate accounting engines.
