# Feature inventory

## Authentication and identity
Login, registration, registration review/status, email verification/OTP, resend OTP, forgot/reset password, profile, avatar, password change, sessions, trusted devices, login history, 2FA setup/verify/toggle/disable/backup codes.

## Workspace and meals
Dashboard, meal configuration, resident meal selection, meal history, overrides, presets, leave, guest meals, kitchen counts/operations, calendar/holidays.

## Finance and accounting
Resident funds, products, purchases, expenses, payments, refunds, adjustments, bills, billing cycles, monthly closing, variables, formula engine, formula versions, ledger/history and reporting.

## Communications and administration
Notifications, announcements, users/residents, settings, policies, personalization, audit logs, background tasks/system tools, data export, roles/permissions and institution settings.

No feature in this inventory may disappear merely because its source implementation is messy.
